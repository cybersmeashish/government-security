## Proposed categorization record

For the fictional citizen-service case, the design hypothesis is moderate confidentiality, moderate integrity and moderate availability impact because inappropriate disclosure or changed/delayed records could materially affect service outcomes. This is a synthetic assumption, not a FIPS categorization determination. No classified information is present.

## Collection gaps and review trigger

The current preliminary USC page was unavailable, and the attempted 2025 PDF returned an HTML error document. Both failures are recorded rather than treated as current statutory evidence. CISA's maturity-model page/PDF returned access errors during collection; they are not used as retrieved authority here.

Before agency adoption, the legal/policy owner must establish current statutory text, applicable OMB/agency requirements, system boundary, data obligations, impact category and approved tailoring. Repeat this review when the jurisdiction, mission, data, supplier or source version changes.
