# 02 — Agency security transformation profile

Ashish Srivastava · 19 November 2026 · **Proposed transformation profile**

## Decision

Fund a transition from bounded local research controls to durable, independently assessed agency operations. This profile describes the reference fixture and a proposed target; it is not an assessment of a real agency.

| CSF 2.0 function | Current reference evidence | Proposed target | Accountable role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Govern | Mission owners, action boundaries and decision records | Approved applicability, supplier and exception decisions with review triggers | Agency service director / CISO |
| Identify | Three missions, synthetic records, seven sovereignty dimensions | Complete service/data/dependency inventory and independently supported placement | Records / operations leads |
| Protect | Scoped sessions, local revocation, two-duty handles and signed imports | Production identity, protected keys and durable policy/replay state | Security / operations leads |
| Detect | Two illustrative local rules and evidence freshness states | Coverage measured against realistic threats, protected retention and loss indicators | Agency CSOC lead |
| Respond | Local revoke, import rejection and disconnected workflow decisions | Rehearsed agency communications, decision delegation and escalation | Incident commander |
| Recover | Five tiny fixture restores with actual missing-record enumeration | Stable-storage restore exercises against approved service objectives | Recovery owner |

## Proposed 90-day sequence

Days 1–30: confirm mission, jurisdiction, data, authority, exception and recovery requirements. Days 31–60: implement durable authority and protected trust roots; enforce the selected infrastructure boundaries. Days 61–90: exercise restart, compromise and restoration with realistic workloads; collect independent placement evidence and review residual risks.

Suggested measures are traceable decisions with owners, negative-case coverage, evidence age and verified restoration outcomes. They are not fabricated agency maturity scores. Exit a phase only when its stated acceptance evidence exists.
