# 03 — National capability reference model

Ashish Srivastava · 19 November 2026 · **Original reference framework**

## Original reference model

This model connects national coordination, ministry oversight, agency accountability and local execution without assigning unofficial responsibilities to real institutions. The role labels below require adaptation to the actual statutory and organizational structure.

| Capability | Coordination layer | Agency responsibility | Local execution | Evidence measure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mission and risk governance | Set common outcome language | Own service risks and exceptions | Apply permitted/stopped actions | Decisions with owner and review trigger / decisions sampled |
| Asset and dependency knowledge | Coordinate shared dependency risks | Maintain service/data/supplier inventory | Record actual site dependencies | Critical dependencies with current evidence / critical dependencies |
| Sovereign operations | Define applicable sovereignty requirements | Decide operator, key, supplier and recovery boundaries | Enforce approved site authority | Boundaries with owner and independently reviewed evidence / required boundaries |
| Supplier assurance | Coordinate acquisition expectations | Specify acceptance and support-access terms | Quarantine and admit approved artifacts | Accepted releases with traceable evidence / releases sampled |
| Detection and intelligence | Coordinate useful indicators and reporting | Own coverage and disclosure decisions | Detect locally and mark evidence freshness | Required signals with tested coverage / required signals |
| Incident coordination | Define escalation/communications interfaces | Assign incident command and delegated decisions | Contain using locally authorized actions | Exercises with observed decisions and gaps / exercises completed |
| Continuity and recovery | Coordinate shared recovery dependencies | Approve service recovery objectives | Restore and enumerate missing changes | Approved objectives with realistic restore evidence / approved objectives |
| Skills and research | Support reproducible learning | Allocate skills to mission risks | Publish suitable synthetic experiments | Independently reproduced results / results offered for reproduction |

## Use and uncertainty

Use an owner/evidence matrix before proposing a national capability score. A ratio requires a defined denominator, observation period and collection method. The table proposes measures; this repository does not claim to have measured national capability.

The government lab supports narrow local authority, update, disclosure and restoration questions. National exercises, institutions, funding decisions, legal authority and adoption remain outside its measured scope.
