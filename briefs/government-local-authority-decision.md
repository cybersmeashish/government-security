# 24 — Executive decision: preserve local review, protect public-service authority

Ashish Srivastava · 19 November 2026 · proposed agency decision brief

**Proposed decision:** fund a bounded pilot that permits scoped local review during disconnection while stopping final determinations and unapproved disclosure. Make protected authority and recovery state acceptance gates before operational adoption.

**Mission consequence:** loss of central connectivity need not stop every review action, but it must not silently expand operator, application, supplier or AI authority. The service owner must decide which effects may continue and which require fresh central approval.

**Measured result:** 54 unique government regression tests and 63 isolated scenario expectations passed. Actual HTTP scoped reads and review markers worked; cross-record/cross-agency access and offline finalization were denied. Three encrypted snapshot records, configuration and software keys were restored; one real post-snapshot fixture record was absent.

**Material finding:** an old signed update was accepted after reconstructing an in-memory admission gate. A current report did not establish detection coverage, and matching site claims did not establish placement assurance. These are recorded limitations.

**Next investment:** protected durable identity/approval/replay state and recoverable trust roots; realistic stable-storage restoration including identity state; independently evidenced platform placement/support boundaries; real-model retrieval/tool evaluation.

**Planning assumptions:** an illustrative first pilot allocation is 10–15 engineer-days for authority/state, 8–12 for key/restore work and 6–9 for independent validation. These are proposed person-day assumptions, not a supplier quote, measured delivery effort, agency budget or monetary ROI. Confirm dependencies and scope before cost approval.

**Accountability and release gate:** the agency service owner owns permitted/stopped outcomes; security and operations own authority/keys/update acceptance; records and acquisition owners confirm disclosure, supplier and applicability requirements. Permit operational rollout only after the agency accepts evidence against its current obligations and approved recovery objectives.

**Uncertainty:** one synthetic container, ephemeral software state, deterministic AI and tiny tmpfs data do not establish government production readiness, compliance or accreditation.
