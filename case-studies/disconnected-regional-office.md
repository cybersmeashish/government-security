# Disconnected regional office — continuity with bounded authority

**Synthetic case · actual network-disabled container plus local decision model**

The regional office continues scoped record review and a nonfinal acknowledgment when central connectivity is absent. Final determination stops. Emergency review requires separate local security and operations approval for a specific requester and record; reuse and retargeting are denied.
