# Government reference roadmap

The 24 numbered contributions supply inspectable reference artifacts, original bounded experiments and decision records. Their stage is recorded individually; architecture and policy proposals are not deployed government controls.

## Next operational gates

1. Establish current agency/jurisdiction applicability, impact categorization and approved tailoring.
2. Implement production authentication/transport and protected durable identity, approval and anti-rollback state.
3. Enforce infrastructure/network/storage boundaries on a chosen platform; independently verify placement and support paths.
4. Exercise authority/replay/revocation after restart and compromise, with realistic workloads and uncertain time.
5. Protect trust roots and wrapping material; restore identity and policy as well as records into a realistic target.
6. Evaluate an actual learned model, retrieval index and authorized tools using appropriate quality and attack datasets.
7. Invite independent reproduction and record real adoption separately from this research portfolio.
